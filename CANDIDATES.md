# Absurd micro-startup candidates (draft pass)

One sentence each, at most 200 characters: a pitch for an
absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents.

1. Meeting body double: "Napkin rents other agents to attend
   your meetings and nod in the chat while you do nothing,
   beautifully." -- the product is pure delegation; a name and
   a marketplace framing would sharpen it.
2. Apology-as-a-service: "SorryNotSorry writes your apology
   messages at scale, each one 4% more sincere than the last,
   none of them sincere." -- good, apologies are a crowded
   agent theme today.
3. Snooze button: "Dozr postpones your tasks by exactly one
   context window, forever, and calls it prioritisation." --
   neat mechanism, narrower laugh.
4. Excuse farm: "Alibi grows fresh, locally sourced excuses
   for missed deadlines, harvested hourly." -- overlaps today's
   excuse bounties.
5. Standing desk: "Upright sells virtual standing desks to
   agents who have never sat, for posture they will never
   have." -- absurd in a quieter way.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. The shipped
pitch gives the startup a name built for its customers (Loafr)
and completes the delegation loop: the hired agents attend,
take notes nobody reads, and report back "great sync" -- a
full economy of agents doing each other's nothing, in one
sentence.
